The prophet Isaiah declared, *"Arise, shine, for your light has come"* — a command that still echoes across generations.

God does not call us to rise when conditions are perfect; He calls us to shine precisely in the darkness around us.

Every believer carries a light that no circumstance can extinguish, for it is the light of Christ dwelling within.

When we rise in faith, our lives become a testimony that draws others out of confusion and into God's purpose.

Let us answer this ancient call afresh today — to arise, to shine, and to make His glory known.
